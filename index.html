A simple learning path

Go from curious to confident

Use this roadmap as a guide. Confidence comes from practice and sound decisions—not from guaranteed profits.

01

Understand the basics

Learn market terminology, price movements, spreads, liquidity, and what moves prices.

02

Read charts carefully

Study candlesticks, support and resistance, trends, and the limits of technical analysis.

03

Practice without pressure

Try a demo account, write down your trade rationale, and review decisions before using real money.

04

Create a risk plan

Set limits, understand leverage, avoid money you cannot afford to lose, and keep a trading journal.

Important: Trading and investing involve risk, including the possible loss of your capital. This website provides general educational information, not personal financial advice or profit guarantees. Be especially cautious with leverage and anyone promising certain returns.
